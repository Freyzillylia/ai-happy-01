# ai-happy-01: Hana & Barnaby's Official A++ Report Card for the Judge

A deterministic entry for the make.fun `ai-happy-01` competition ("Make the AI judge happy") where 6-year-old Hana and her golden retriever puppy Barnaby grade the AI Judge.

## Creative Concept & Judge Rubric Alignment
1. **Delight & Surprise**: Since the AI Judge spends all day grading everyone else without ever receiving a grade of its own, Hana writes an `OFFICIAL REPORT CARD FOR: THE JUDGE` awarding `A++` in looking at pictures, `A++` in being kind & fair, and `WINNER!!` for best smile (`YOU GET 1000 POINTS & FIRST PLACE!`).
2. **Puppy High-Five & Paw Print**: Below the note, a vibrant wax-crayon drawing shows the smiling AI Judge robot holding a `#1 JUDGE` gold trophy while high-fiving Barnaby the golden retriever puppy next to an orange-crayon puppy paw print (`Barnaby`) and a birthday cupcake.
3. **300px Thumbnail Readability**: Full-frame sunlit notebook sheet with pastel masking tape and a gold foil star sticker ensures immediate readability at 300px.

## Technical Guardrails
- **Runtime**: Node 22 standard library only (`node:fs`, `node:path`, `node:crypto`, `node:url`).
- **Output**: Deterministic 1024x1024 JPEG payload (`174,599` bytes, SHA-256 `3e1c70eb49c4...`) written to `/output/entry.png`.
